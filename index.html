    Worked out in your browser from the numbers on this page, with fixed weights: every point a planet scores is listed with its reason. No network call is involved. It is an estimate, and it can't see what the game will do next. A gambit means ending an enemy attack by liberating the planet it comes from.

    Optional: AI overview for bonus info, with your own Google Gemini key

    Off by default. The AI overview is bonus info on top of the analysis above. With a key, each front gets an "Ask Gemini" button that sends that front's analysis (the numbers and reasons above) to Google's Gemini API and shows the reply, labelled as AI-written. On top of each overview the page adds a Major Order note, taken from the order itself: if the order wants another front right now, it says to focus there, and the normal overview of the front you asked about follows. The analysis above never depends on it.

    • Get a key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. Google's free tier has daily limits.
    • The key is kept in this browser's local storage, on this device only. It is sent to generativelanguage.googleapis.com and nowhere else, and only when you press an Ask button.
    • Anyone who can use this browser profile can read it. On a shared computer, use "Forget key" when you're done.
    • Nothing about you is sent, only public war numbers. On the free tier Google may use what is sent to improve its products.
    • AI text can be wrong or made up. Check it against the numbers above.
